Who we are

Meadow is provided by Surya Penaganti (“we”, “us”). We decide how the personal data described here is used — under GDPR we are the “controller”, and under India’s DPDP Act the “data fiduciary”.

Questions, requests or complaints about your data: email spdevac45@gmail.com. This contact is also our grievance officer for users in India. We reply within 30 days.

What we collect, and why

Here is everything, item by item. Most of it never leaves your phone.

Your account

What
Email address, the name you choose, and your Apple or Google sign-in ID if you use one (Google may also pass on your profile name and photo link). When your account was created, and standard security logs of sign-ins such as time and IP address.
Why
To create your account, sign you in, reset your password and let you delete your account.
Where
With our sign-in provider, Supabase, on their servers.
Basis
Needed to provide the service you asked for (contract). Required — without it you can’t sign in.

Your logs

What
Meals and nutrition, mood check-ins (including emotions, tags, sleep and anything you write in your journal), workouts and sets, weigh-ins, goals, training days and your app settings.
Why
To show your day, your history, streaks, goals and patterns.
Where
Only on your phone, in Meadow’s own storage. We never receive a copy.
Basis
Your choice to enter it. Some of it (mood, sleep, weight) is health-related, so we treat it as sensitive: it is used only for your own features, never for anything else.

Steps and heart rate (optional)

What
Daily step totals and resting heart rate for the last 28 days, read from Apple Health or Health Connect after you turn on “Steps & heart rate”. Meadow only reads — it never writes to Health.
Why
To find patterns between your activity and your mood, sleep and training.
Where
Copied onto your phone only. Never uploaded, never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, and never stored in iCloud by Meadow.
Basis
Your consent, given in the system permission screen. Turn it off any time in the AI screen or in your phone’s Health settings.

Camera

What
The live camera view while scanning, and still photos when reading a nutrition label.
Why
To read barcodes and nutrition labels.
Where
Read on your phone. Label photos are deleted straight after reading. Nothing is uploaded.

Barcode numbers

What
The number printed under a barcode you scan.
Why
To look up the product’s nutrition.
Where
Sent to Open Food Facts, a free public food database, which also sees your internet (IP) address like any website does. Nothing about you or your logs is sent. Results are saved on your phone for 30 days, so the same barcode isn’t looked up again.

Voice search (optional)

What
What you say after tapping the microphone.
Why
To type your search for you.
Where
Turned into text on your phone when it supports that. If it doesn’t, we ask you once before using Apple’s or Google’s speech service, which then receives the audio under their own privacy policy.

The AI model

What
A one-time download of the Gemma model (about 1.5 GB).
Why
So Meadow · AI can run on your phone.
Where
Downloaded from Hugging Face, which sees your IP address like any download. After that the AI works offline. Your logs are never sent to it or to anyone.

Meadow+ purchases

What
Whether you have an active subscription.
Why
To unlock Meadow+.
Where
Payments are handled by the App Store or Google Play. We never see your card details.

Messages to us

What
Your email address and what you write, if you contact support.
Why
To reply to you.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos library, advertising ID, analytics or crash reports. (The “Crash reports” switch in Privacy only saves your preference for now — no crash data is collected.)

Notifications and reminders

Reminders, the streak alert and new-pattern alerts are scheduled on your phone. We don’t run a push-notification server and don’t receive anything when a notification is shown. Turn any of them off in You → Notifications, or all of them in your phone’s settings.

How Meadow · AI works

Meadow looks for links in your own logs over the last 28 days — for example, sleep and next-day mood — and checks each one against the raw numbers. Only findings that are at least 60% certain are shown. The on-device Gemma model then puts them into words.

This is automated processing, but it never makes decisions about you with legal or similarly significant effects. Patterns are correlations, not medical advice. Your “Yes / Not really” answers stay on your phone and can be cleared with “Clear AI memory”.

A small background task may run overnight to refresh your patterns and inbox. It uses the same on-device data and sends nothing anywhere.

Who we share data with

We don’t sell or rent your personal data, share it for targeted advertising, or give it to data brokers. The only other parties involved are the services that make the features above work:

  • Supabase — hosts sign-in and your account record.
  • Apple and Google — Sign in with Apple / Google, App Store / Google Play purchases, Apple Health / Health Connect on your phone, and speech-to-text only if you agree to it.
  • Open Food Facts — receives barcode numbers.
  • Hugging Face — serves the AI model download.

We may also disclose data if the law requires it, for example to answer a valid court order — and only what is required.

Where data is processed

Your logs stay on your phone. The services above may process data in other countries, including the United States, where it may be accessible to courts and authorities under that country’s law. Where the law requires it, they use recognised safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long we keep it

  • Data on your phone: until you delete it, use Clear all data, delete your account, or uninstall Meadow.
  • Your account: until you delete it in Privacy → Delete account. It is removed from our sign-in provider straight away; routine provider backups are overwritten within a short period.
  • Support messages: as long as needed to help you, then deleted.

How we protect it

Your phone encrypts its storage, and Meadow’s data sits in the app’s private area that other apps can’t read. You can add a Face ID lock in Privacy. Everything that does travel over the internet uses encrypted connections (HTTPS).

If a breach ever affects your data, we will tell you and the relevant authorities within the time the law requires.

Your rights and choices

Wherever you live, you can:

  • See and export your data — You → Export data (CSV, JSON or PDF).
  • Correct it — edit or delete any entry.
  • Delete it — Privacy → Clear all data, or Delete account for everything.
  • Withdraw consent — turn off Steps & heart rate, the camera, microphone or notifications at any time. This doesn’t affect anything done before.
  • Ask us anything about your data, or object to how it’s used — email spdevac45@gmail.com.

Canada: under PIPEDA and, in Quebec, Law 25, you can access and correct the personal information we hold about you and withdraw consent, and we reply within 30 days. Surya Penaganti is accountable for how Meadow handles personal information and is the person in charge of protecting it under Quebec law. To reach them, email spdevac45@gmail.com. If you’re not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or, in Quebec, the Commission d’accès à l’information.

EU and UK: you also have the rights to restriction and portability, and to complain to your local data protection authority.

India: you can nominate someone to exercise your rights if you die or can’t, and you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India after contacting our grievance officer.

US states (including California and Washington’s My Health My Data Act): you can confirm, access and delete your consumer health data and withdraw consent. We don’t sell it or use it for targeted ads.

We never charge for requests and won’t treat you differently for using your rights.

Children

Meadow is for adults aged 18 and over. We don’t knowingly collect data from children. If you think a child has created an account, contact us and we’ll delete it.

Not a medical service

Meadow is a wellness app. It isn’t a medical device and doesn’t diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. In an emergency, contact your local emergency number.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we use your data, we’ll update this page and tell you in the app before the change takes effect. If a change needs your consent, we’ll ask for it.
